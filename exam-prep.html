Exam Prep & Academic English Instructor

Jon is a native English-speaking instructor with an MA in Applied Linguistics from the University of Birmingham and more than 10 years of experience preparing students for major standardized exams, including TOEFL, IELTS, SAT, ACT, GRE, and GMAT.

He currently teaches academic English at Long Island University and Adelphi University International, working with international students on the language and academic skills they need to succeed at U.S. universities.

His professional background also includes curriculum development, instructional design, teacher training, and educational content development.
