2 · Learn through dialogue

A New Yorker gives directions

Complete the dialogue. Notice how the phrases are used naturally.

Leo: How long is your from Brooklyn to work?
Nina: About 45 minutes. I take the G, then to the 7.
Leo: And your office is or downtown?
Nina: Midtown, actually. The station is only from my office.
