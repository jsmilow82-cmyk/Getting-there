Paraphrasing

Learn how to express information from a source in your own words while keeping the original meaning.

What is paraphrasing?

A paraphrase expresses the same idea as the original source, but uses different language and sentence structure.

  • Keep the original meaning.
  • Change vocabulary where appropriate.
  • Change the sentence structure.
  • Keep approximately the same level of detail.
  • Write naturally and clearly.
Remember: changing a few words to synonyms is not enough. A paraphrased source still needs to be cited.
Exercise 1

Which is the better paraphrase?

Choose the version that changes both the wording and structure while preserving the original meaning.

Original

Many university students experience stress because they have to balance academic responsibilities, work, and their personal lives.

Original

Regular physical activity can improve mental health by reducing anxiety and improving mood.

Original

Social media allows people to communicate across long distances, but excessive use may also contribute to feelings of isolation.

Exercise 2

Change the structure

Rewrite each sentence using the beginning provided.

Original

Because housing costs have increased, many students are choosing to live with their parents while attending university.

Begin with: Rising housing costs...

Model answer Rising housing costs have led many university students to continue living with their parents while they study.
Original

Students who regularly participate in class discussions often become more confident speakers.

Begin with: Regular participation...

Model answer Regular participation in classroom discussions can help students become more confident when speaking.
Original

Universities are introducing more online courses because students increasingly want flexible study options.

Begin with: Students' growing demand...

Model answer Students' growing demand for flexible study options has encouraged universities to offer more online courses.
Exercise 3

Guided paraphrasing

Rewrite each sentence in your own words. Use the suggestions if they help, but you do not have to use all of them.

Original

International students may struggle during their first semester because they have to adjust to both a new academic system and a new culture.

face difficulties · adapt · unfamiliar academic system · cultural environment

Model paraphrase International students may face difficulties during their first semester as they adapt to an unfamiliar academic system and cultural environment.
Original

Getting enough sleep is important for students because sleep affects concentration, memory, and academic performance.

adequate sleep · ability to focus · remember information · academic performance

Model paraphrase Adequate sleep is essential for students because it influences their ability to concentrate, remember information, and perform well academically.
Original

Students who develop effective time-management skills are often better able to meet deadlines and avoid unnecessary stress.

manage time effectively · complete work on time · reduce stress

Model paraphrase Students who learn to manage their time effectively are more likely to complete their work on schedule and experience less unnecessary stress.
Exercise 4

Paraphrase independently

Paraphrase each sentence without prompts. Change both the language and sentence structure while keeping the original meaning.

Original

Young adults are increasingly delaying major life decisions such as getting married, buying a home, and having children.

Model paraphrase An increasing number of young adults are waiting until later in life to marry, purchase homes, and start families.
Original

Learning a second language can help people understand different cultures and communicate with a wider range of people.

Model paraphrase Studying another language can broaden people's understanding of other cultures and allow them to communicate with more people.
Original

The growing cost of higher education has caused many students to question whether a university degree is worth the financial investment.

Model paraphrase As higher education becomes more expensive, many students are beginning to reconsider whether the financial cost of earning a degree is worthwhile.
Original

Working from home gives employees greater flexibility, although some people find it more difficult to separate their professional and personal lives.

Model paraphrase Remote work can provide employees with more flexibility, but it may also make it harder for them to maintain a clear boundary between work and their private lives.
Exercise 5

Paraphrase short passages

Paraphrase each passage. Keep the important information while changing the language and structure.

Source A

College students often experience changes in their social relationships after beginning university. They may spend less time with friends from high school while forming new relationships with classmates and roommates. These changes can be exciting, but they can also make some students feel lonely during the transition.

Model paraphrase Starting university often changes students' social lives. They may have less contact with old school friends while developing new friendships with classmates and people they live with. Although this transition can be exciting, it may also lead to feelings of loneliness.
Source B

Smartphones have made it possible for people to access information almost anywhere and at any time. However, constant access to digital information may make it more difficult for people to concentrate on a single task for long periods.

Model paraphrase Smartphones allow users to obtain information at nearly any place or time. At the same time, continuous access to digital content may reduce people's ability to focus on one activity for an extended period.
Source C

Part-time employment can provide university students with valuable professional experience and additional income. At the same time, working too many hours may reduce the amount of time students have available for studying and completing assignments.

Model paraphrase Having a part-time job can give university students useful work experience as well as extra money. However, spending excessive time at work can leave them with less time for coursework and studying.
