Paraphrasing

Learn how to express information from a source in your own words while keeping the original meaning.

What is paraphrasing?

A paraphrase expresses the same idea as the original source, but uses different language and sentence structure.

A good paraphrase should:

  • keep the original meaning;
  • use your own vocabulary where appropriate;
  • change the sentence structure, not only individual words;
  • include approximately the same level of detail as the original;
  • be written naturally and clearly.
Remember: Changing a few words to synonyms is not enough. Even when you paraphrase successfully, you still need to cite the original source in academic writing.
Exercise 1

Which is the better paraphrase?

Read the original sentence and the two versions below it. Choose the version that paraphrases the idea more effectively.

Original

Many university students experience stress because they have to balance academic responsibilities, work, and their personal lives.

Original

Regular physical activity can improve mental health by reducing anxiety and improving mood.

Original

Social media allows people to communicate across long distances, but excessive use may also contribute to feelings of isolation.

Exercise 2

Change the structure

Paraphrasing is not just replacing vocabulary. Practice changing the grammatical structure while keeping the same meaning.

Original

Because housing costs have increased, many students are choosing to live with their parents while attending university.

Begin your sentence with:

Rising housing costs...

Original

Students who regularly participate in class discussions often become more confident speakers.

Begin your sentence with:

Regular participation...

Original

Universities are introducing more online courses because students increasingly want flexible study options.

Begin your sentence with:

Students' growing demand...

Exercise 3

Guided paraphrasing

Rewrite each sentence in your own words. The prompts underneath give you ideas for how you could change the language or structure. You do not have to use every suggestion.

Original

International students may struggle during their first semester because they have to adjust to both a new academic system and a new culture.

You might use: face difficulties · adapt · unfamiliar · cultural environment

Original

Getting enough sleep is important for students because sleep affects concentration, memory, and academic performance.

You might use: adequate sleep · ability to focus · remember information · perform academically

Original

Students who develop effective time-management skills are often better able to meet deadlines and avoid unnecessary stress.

You might use: manage their time effectively · complete work on time · reduce stress

Exercise 4

Paraphrase independently

Now paraphrase each sentence without prompts. Try to change both the vocabulary and the structure while preserving the original meaning.

Original

Young adults are increasingly delaying major life decisions such as getting married, buying a home, and having children.

Original

Learning a second language can help people understand different cultures and communicate with a wider range of people.

Original

The growing cost of higher education has caused many students to question whether a university degree is worth the financial investment.

Original

Working from home gives employees greater flexibility, although some people find it more difficult to separate their professional and personal lives.

Exercise 5

Paraphrase short passages

Paraphrase each short passage. Keep all of the important information, but express the ideas using your own language and sentence structure.

Source A

College students often experience changes in their social relationships after beginning university. They may spend less time with friends from high school while forming new relationships with classmates and roommates. These changes can be exciting, but they can also make some students feel lonely during the transition.

Source B

Smartphones have made it possible for people to access information almost anywhere and at any time. However, constant access to digital information may make it more difficult for people to concentrate on a single task for long periods.

Source C

Part-time employment can provide university students with valuable professional experience and additional income. At the same time, working too many hours may reduce the amount of time students have available for studying and completing assignments.
